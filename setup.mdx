title: "Setup"
---

The setup page lists all the required steps needed to configure and launch your store. You can also manage these settings anytime from your dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your model">
    Select how you want to accept payments and run your store:
    - **All-in-One (5% + 50¢ per transaction, \$0/mo):** Blink acts as your Merchant of Record, handles global tax calculation and remittance, includes Affiliates and Revenue Splits, and pays out your earnings via Stripe Express.
    - **Own Stripe (\$49/mo or \$490/yr, 0% Blink transaction fee):** Connect your own Standard Stripe account directly so 100% of sales go straight to your Stripe balance. Build and test for free in Test Mode and subscribe only when you're ready to go live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your store">
    Add your store logo, support email, and set your refund policy (plus your business invoice details if using **Own Stripe**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first product">
    Simple products are standalone items with no options, featuring only one price\
    (e.g., a Framer template, Icon pack, ebook)

    [More about adding products](/add-products)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect payouts or Stripe account">
    - **All-in-One:** Complete your KYC and add your bank details via Stripe Express.
    - **Own Stripe:** Connect your Standard Stripe account via OAuth in one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    - **All-in-One:** Submit your store for review. We’ll check everything within 8 hours, then you’re ready to start selling.
    - **Own Stripe:** Activate your monthly (\$49/mo) or yearly (\$490/yr) subscription to go live immediately with 0% Blink fees.
  </Step>
</Steps>
