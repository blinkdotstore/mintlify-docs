## Product types

The info tab is where you add basic product into<br /><br />**One-time**

One-time products are purchased once with a single payment. Ideal for digital products, services, templates, ebooks, courses, software licenses, and other items that don’t require recurring billing.

**Subscription**

Subscriptions charge customers automatically on a recurring schedule such as weekly, monthly, or yearly. Ideal for memberships, communities, SaaS products, newsletters, retainers, and ongoing services.

**Bundles**

Bundles allow you to group multiple existing products together and sell them as a single package, often at a discounted rate. Ideal for creating ultimate toolkits, premium collections, and promotional packages to incentivize customers and increase your average order value.

## Pricing

<Accordion title="**Pricing model**">
  Choose how you want to bill customers. Select **Standard pricing** for one-time purchases, **Subscriptions** for recurring billing schedules, or **Bundles** to package multiple existing products together into a single discounted offer.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Currency**">
  The base settlement currency for your product. All list prices, discounts, and checkout totals calculate from this baseline before any localized or multi-currency conversions apply.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Price**">
  The baseline retail amount charged. For one-time products and bundles, this is the total upfront checkout cost; for recurring products, it serves as the base amount billed at the start of each cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Billing interval & Free trial**">
  _(Subscriptions only)_ Define how often your customer is charged (e.g., every 1 month, 3 months, or 1 year). You can also add an optional trial period (in days) to grant access before the first automated charge runs.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Included products**">
  _(Bundles only)_ Select the individual products packaged inside this bundle. Deliverables, entitlements, and access permissions associated with each selected product are granted simultaneously upon purchase.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Automatic discount**">
  Apply a storewide or product-specific discount rule directly to the checkout flow. Eligible buyers receive the price reduction instantly without needing to manually input a promo code.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Seat pricing**">
  Charge per individual user or seat on top of your base price. Ideal for SaaS, multi-user software licenses, or agency asset packs that scale based on team size.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Metered billing**">
  _(Subscriptions only)_ Bill customers dynamically based on real-time usage (such as API calls, bandwidth, or cloud storage) tracked and synced from your application alongside the subscription period.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**Automate revocation**">
  Automatically remove customer deliverables and access (including Discord roles, active license keys, or software entitlements) whenever a subscription expires, is canceled, or fails payment retries.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="**No deliverables**">
  Bypass built-in file downloads and license keys if order fulfillment is handled externally. Blink will confirm the transaction and trigger order details directly to your system via Webhooks or the Blink API.
</Accordion>

## Settings

Variable products allow customers to choose options like size or color on one page, with unique prices, SKUs, and stock levels for each combination.

<Accordion title="Cart recovery">
  Recover lost sales automatically with a 3-step abandoned checkout flow. When a customer drops off, we send a series of high-converting emails to bring them back complete with built-in incentives. The second and third emails include a discount to nudge them over the line.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Geo Pricing">
  Dynamically adapt your product pricing to your customer's local purchasing power parity (PPP). When enabled, buyers in supported countries automatically see localized, optimized rates to boost international conversions without hurting margins in higher-income markets.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Upsells">
  Maximize your average order value by presenting high-converting, one-click post-purchase or checkout offers. Toggle this off if you want this specific product to remain a standalone checkout experience with no additional upsell prompts shown to the buyer.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Bumps">
  Order bumps are a simple way to increase your checkout value. They appear right before the Pay button and let customers quickly add extras to their order with a single click.

  They’re perfect for offering complementary add-ons like extended support, setup services, or premium extras.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Visibility">
  Choose how you want your product to be discovered. You can make it visible on your storefront for anyone to browse, or keep it private and share it only via direct link.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  Blink only allows adding digital products you own, adding a product that infringes copyrights may result in immediate termination of your account.
</Note>
