Automate your workflows by connecting Blink Store to over 5,000+ apps on Zapier. With our Zapier integration, you can automatically trigger actions in other apps whenever important events happen in your store.

## Getting Started

To start building automations with Blink Store on Zapier:

1. Log in to your [Zapier account](https://zapier.com) and click **Create a Zap**.
2. In the trigger step, search for **Blink Store**.
3. Select the trigger event you want to use (e.g., *Order Created*).
4. Click **Continue** and sign in to your Blink Store account when prompted. You will need to provide your **API Key** (which you can generate in your Blink Store Developer Settings).
5. Set up your action step with any other app on Zapier (like Gmail, Slack, or Google Sheets).

## Available Triggers

Blink Store offers a comprehensive set of triggers to help you automate every aspect of your business. 

### Orders & Checkouts
- **Order Created**: Triggers when a new order is placed.
- **Order Refunded**: Triggers when an order is refunded.
- **Checkout Created**: Triggers when a checkout session is created.
- **Checkout Completed**: Triggers when a checkout is completed.
- **Checkout Expired**: Triggers when a checkout session expires.

### Customers & Subscriptions
- **Customer Created**: Triggers when a new customer is created.
- **Customer Updated**: Triggers when customer details are updated.
- **Subscriber Added**: Triggers when a new subscriber joins the audience.
- **Seat Added**: Triggers when a seat is assigned to a member.
- **Seat Removed**: Triggers when a seat is removed from a member.
- **Subscription Created**: Triggers when a subscription is created.
- **Subscription Updated**: Triggers when a subscription is updated.
- **Subscription Trial Ended**: Triggers when a subscription trial ends.
- **Subscription Canceled**: Triggers when a subscription is canceled.
- **Subscription Expired**: Triggers when a subscription expires.
- **Subscription Past Due**: Triggers when a subscription becomes past due.

### Products & Discounts
- **Product Created**: Triggers when a product is created.
- **Product Updated**: Triggers when a product is updated.
- **Product Archived**: Triggers when a product is archived.
- **Bundle Created**: Triggers when a bundle is created.
- **Bundle Updated**: Triggers when a bundle is updated.
- **Bundle Archived**: Triggers when a bundle is archived.
- **Discount Created**: Triggers when a discount is created.
- **Discount Updated**: Triggers when a discount is updated.
- **Discount Deleted**: Triggers when a discount is deleted.

### Growth Tools (Upsells & Bumps)
- **Upsell Created**: Triggers when an upsell is created.
- **Upsell Updated**: Triggers when an upsell is updated.
- **Upsell Deleted**: Triggers when an upsell is deleted.
- **Upsell Claimed**: Triggers when an upsell is claimed.
- **Bump Paid**: Triggers when a bump is purchased.
- **Abandoned Cart Created**: Triggers when a cart is abandoned.
- **Abandoned Cart Recovered**: Triggers when an abandoned cart is recovered.

### Affiliates & Splits
- **Affiliate Created**: Triggers when an affiliate application is created.
- **Affiliate Approved**: Triggers when an affiliate application is approved.
- **Affiliate Conversion**: Triggers when an affiliate records a conversion.
- **Affiliate Payout Processed**: Triggers when an affiliate payout is processed.
- **Split Created**: Triggers when a split is created.
- **Split Updated**: Triggers when a split is updated.
- **Split Deleted**: Triggers when a split is deleted.

### Financials
- **Payout Created**: Triggers when a payout is created.
- **Payout Paid**: Triggers when a payout is successfully paid.
- **Payout Failed**: Triggers when a payout fails.
- **Payout Canceled**: Triggers when a payout is canceled.

### Example Automations

- **Slack Notifications**: Send a message to a Slack channel every time an *Order is Created*.
- **Email Marketing**: Add a customer to a Mailchimp or ConvertKit sequence when a *Subscriber is Added*.
- **Spreadsheet Tracking**: Log all *Affiliate Conversions* into a Google Sheet for easy tracking.
